# UC4 - Maintain Ordered Bogie Consist

## Objective
Maintain ordered sequence of train bogies.

## Problem in UC3
HashSet ensures uniqueness but does not preserve order.

## Solution
Use LinkedList to maintain sequence and allow efficient insertions.

## Concepts Used
- LinkedList
- add()
- add(index, element)
- remove()
- Order preservation

## Outcome
Train consist maintains correct physical sequence.
